Lyra Neural Verse

A language model built from first principles that writes Vietnamese verse.

Source on GitHub

Write

A small transformer trained on Truyện Kiều picks each syllable; a constrained decoder only lets it pick syllables that keep the lục bát tone and rhyme rules.

Loading the model...

B bằng and T trắc mark the positions the rules constrain; rhyme marks rhyming syllables. Hover a syllable for its probability among the syllables the rules allowed.

Check

Paste lục bát (one line per row, or separated by "/") to check tones and rhymes.

Everything runs in your browser: the int8 model file, the forward pass and the decoder. No server, no tracking.
